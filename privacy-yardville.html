YardVille: Jamaica Farm Town – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026. YardVille is made by Bright Spark Games (com.brightsparkgames.yardville).

What we collect

YardVille does not ask for your name, email address or an account. Your farm progress is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers that store information about you.

Advertising

The game offers optional reward videos. Ads are served by Google AdMob, with Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) taking part in AdMob mediation. These companies may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location based on your IP address, device information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. Their policies: Google (policies.google.com/privacy), Unity (unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy), Liftoff (liftoff.io/privacy-policy).

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Internet checks

To keep timers fair, the game reads the current time from public internet servers (Google and Cloudflare), and it downloads a list of valid coupon codes from our website. These requests do not include any personal information.

Children

YardVille is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and deleting data

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and limit ad personalisation in your device settings. Uninstalling the game deletes all of its data from your device.
